Who we are

The Haitian Association of Interpreters and Translators International (HAITI) is the premier global organization dedicated to uniting and empowering Haitian language professionals worldwide.

We are built on the fundamental belief that interpreters and translators are vital architects of understanding. In a world rich with diversity yet divided by language barriers, our members are the crucial link that forges connection, fosters clarity, and promotes the vibrant tapestry of Haitian culture.

“HAITI is more than an association; we are a collective voice, a resource for excellence, and a home for those who are committed to the power of language and the pride of Haitian heritage.”

Haitian Association of Interpreters and Translators International
